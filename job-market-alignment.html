TIB ACADEMY · COURSE DESIGN EVIDENCE

Why This Course Matches the Job

TIB ILA was not assembled from random controls topics. Its learning architecture was designed from the work a Controls Lead / Controls Manager must perform, then challenged against current SAP Security, GRC, controls, audit and governance job requirements.

The Design Strategy

CURRENT ROLE DEMAND → REQUIRED CAPABILITIES → PROJECT ARTIFACTS → PRACTICE SCENARIOS → LEADERSHIP JUDGMENT → EMPLOYMENT-READINESS DEFENSE

1 · Read the market

Identify recurring responsibilities in current role descriptions: SOX/ITGC, risk and control effectiveness, SoD/access governance, audit readiness, S/4HANA/Fiori/cloud awareness, remediation, automation, reporting and stakeholder leadership.

2 · Convert demand into work

Translate those requirements into the actual inventories, RCMs, owner matrices, evidence registers, test plans, workpapers, deficiency logs, dashboards and leadership decisions used on a controls engagement.

3 · Simulate the job

Use one continuous Corvane Energy environment so the learner works through realistic systems, interfaces, people, risks, controls, evidence, audit pressure and management decisions.

4 · Test readiness

Remove much of the guidance and require the candidate to assess, prioritize, delegate, challenge, review, decide, escalate, report and defend.

Why the Boundary Matters

Some advertisements combine two jobs: controls/governance leadership and hands-on SAP Security/GRC engineering. TIB ILA deliberately trains the first role while giving the manager enough technical literacy to challenge the second. It does not misrepresent a Controls Lead as a PFCG, SU24, MSMP, BRF+ or GRC-configuration engineer.

What This Means for the Trainee

You should be able to look at a current job statement of work and recognize the work because you have already practiced the underlying chain: understand the environment, define scope, identify risk, map controls, establish ownership, obtain and validate evidence, plan and review testing, manage deficiencies, challenge technical assertions, manage audit, lead people and communicate the controls position.

THE COURSE IS BUILT BACKWARD FROM THE JOB — NOT FORWARD FROM A TEXTBOOK.
